Netflora Online

Smart species detection on orthophotos

A concise overview of the project and the technical demo for forest inventory with artificial intelligence support.

Open Streamlit demo Switch language

The Streamlit demo may take a few minutes to load and process the orthophoto.

Project goal

Automate the identification of vegetation species on high-resolution orthophotos, producing maps and tables to support forest inventories and decision making.

Problem context

Manual counting and classification across large areas is slow, expensive, and subject to operational inconsistencies.

Condensed solution

A tiling pipeline with YOLO-based detection and georeferencing to deliver structured results and map visualization.

Main outputs

Detection CSV, interactive map, and optional shapefile for GIS workflows.

Technical highlights

  • YOLO detection models tailored for target species.
  • Orthophoto tiling with controlled overlap.
  • Coordinate conversion to build WGS84 polygons.
  • Interactive map with orthophoto overlay and detections.
  • Simple export to CSV and result package.

AI, computer vision, and geoprocessing

The flow combines image processing, neural inference, and spatial transforms to deliver indicators and maps ready for analysis.
